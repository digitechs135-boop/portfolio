HOW I WORK

A considered path from question to interface.

01 / Understand

Start with people and context.

User-centered design, user flows, usability testing, and behavior analysis help frame the problem before choosing a solution.

02 / Shape

Make the experience tangible.

Wireframes and prototypes bring ideas into focus, helping teams explore interactions and make decisions early.

03 / Deliver

Bring clarity into the details.

Responsive UI, visual systems, and close collaboration with product and engineering carry the work through handoff.
